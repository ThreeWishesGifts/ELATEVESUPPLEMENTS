Our story

Women built this. Women run this. Women were tired of guessing.

Elateve Wellness started the way most honest things do — with a counter full of half-used bottles and a question nobody could answer simply: what do I actually take right now?

Every legacy option seemed to treat women's health as one long, flat age bracket, or split it into confusing categories that didn't map to real life — cycles, pregnancy, the exhausted middle years, perimenopause, and the decades after. So we built the thing we wished existed: one gummy a day that changes with you, reviewed by clinicians, and honest about what's actually inside.

We're a women-founded, women-led team, and we're building Elateve for the version of this industry that should have existed all along — one built on transparency and ease, not a thousand pills a day.
