The product system

Five biological phases. One continuous system.

Legacy wellness brands sell by age bracket — as if 22 and 34 need the same thing, or as if 46 stops needing anything at all. Your body moves through distinct biological phases. Elateve moves with it.

Phase 01

Flow

Active cycle · ages 18–35

Cycle regularity, stress resilience, and hormonal balance through the reproductive years.

Key actives: Myo-Inositol, Folate, Zinc, KSM-66® Ashwagandha

Phase 02

Bloom

The maternal window

Nourishment built for pregnancy and postpartum — for both of you.

Key actives: Choline, Iron Bisglycinate, Algal DHA

Phase 03

Thrive

Burnout recovery

For the season when you're running everyone else's life on empty. Energy and stress recovery, rebuilt from the inside.

Key actives: CoQ10, Magnesium Glycinate, Rhodiola

Phase 04

Shift

Perimenopause · ages 40–50

Support through the hormonal swings, hot flashes, and mood shifts of the transition.

Key actives: Black Cohosh, Vitex, Affron® Saffron

Phase 05

Wisdom

Post-menopause · 50+

Bone density, cognitive clarity, and vitality for the chapter after menopause.

Key actives: Calcium Hydroxyapatite, Maca, Lion's Mane

Our story

Women built this. Women run this. Women were tired of guessing.

Elateve Wellness started the way most honest things do — with a counter full of half-used bottles and a question nobody could answer simply: what do I actually take right now?

Every legacy option seemed to treat women's health as one long, flat age bracket, or split it into confusing categories that didn't map to real life — cycles, pregnancy, the exhausted middle years, perimenopause, and the decades after. So we built the thing we wished existed: a system that changes with you, reviewed by clinicians, and honest about what's actually inside.

We're a women-founded, women-led team, and we're building Elateve for the version of this industry that should have existed all along — one built on transparency and ease, not a thousand pills a day.
